Jiamin Zhao

Ph.D. Candidate in Accounting
IESE Business School

I study how firms respond when stakeholders and emerging technologies reshape the flow and use of corporate information.

My research focuses on corporate disclosure, public activism, and the governance of digital information access.

Job Market Paper / Working paper

Public Activism and Labor-Related Corporate Disclosure

Single-authored · October 2026 draft

Greater local labor-related public activism is associated with less discussion of current labor operating conditions relative to talent-management information in earnings calls.

Award The Sue Hrasky Best PhD Paper Prize, BAFA, 2026.

Working paper

NGO Activism, Corporate Disclosure, and Real Responses

With Simon Xu (Stanford) · October 2026 draft

Environmental NGO targeting is associated with more managerial discussion of climate-transition topics relative to renewable-energy technologies, but lower subsequent demand for specialized green-technology workers.

Working paper

How Firms Govern Automated Access to Corporate Information: Evidence from Robots.txt

With Long Cheng (IESE) and Igor Kadach (IESE) · November 2026 draft

Archived robots.txt records show widespread general access restrictions, while prior crawler-specific policies are associated with later restrictions on newly introduced AI-related identifiers.
